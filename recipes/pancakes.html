Pancakes

Description

Pancakes are a simple and tasty breakfast dish. They are soft, fluffy, and made with basic ingredients like flour, milk, and eggs. They can be served with honey, butter, or fresh fruits.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • A pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Take a bowl and mix flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  2. Add the egg, milk, and melted butter.
  3. Mix everything until you get a smooth batter.
  4. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour a small amount of batter into the pan.
  6. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface.
  7. Flip the pancake and cook the other side until golden.
  8. Repeat with the remaining batter.
  9. Serve warm with honey or your favourite topping.

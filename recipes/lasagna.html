Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a popular Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese. It is baked until the top becomes golden and the cheese melts.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • Ground meat
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and cook the chopped onion and garlic.
  3. Add ground meat and cook until it turns brown.
  4. Add tomato sauce, salt, and pepper. Let it cook for a few minutes.
  5. Place a layer of lasagna sheets in a baking dish.
  6. Add the meat sauce and cheese on top.
  7. Repeat the layers until all the ingredients are used.
  8. Bake for about 30–40 minutes until the cheese is golden.
  9. Let it cool slightly before serving.

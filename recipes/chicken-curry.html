Chicken Curry

Description

Chicken curry is a well-known dish made with chicken, onions, tomatoes, and spices. It has a rich gravy and is usually served with rice or roti. It is a great choice for a homemade meal.

Ingredients

  • 500 grams chicken
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • Water as needed

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add chopped onions and cook until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for a minute.
  4. Add chopped tomatoes and all the spices.
  5. Cook until the tomatoes become soft.
  6. Add the chicken and mix it well with the masala.
  7. Cook for a few minutes, stirring occasionally.
  8. Add water, cover the pan, and cook until the chicken is fully cooked.
  9. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves.
  10. Serve hot with rice or roti.
